# Core human desires — B2C calibration

Loaded by `iv-desire-evaluator` together with `demand-drivers.md` (in this
same directory), which aggregates these ten desires into the five scored
dimensions. This file defines the desires; the scoring mechanism lives in
the agent brief. Successful consumer products activate several desires at
once — that is why the pricing pack pays a secondary-driver bonus.

| # | Desire | What it covers | Functional note | Scored under |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Survival & Physical Security | Food, shelter, health, safety, stability, financial security | The base layer: if unmet, higher desires lose pull | Survival |
| 2 | Pleasure & Pain Avoidance | Comfort, rest, enjoyment; avoiding stress, boredom, effort | Everyday app choices are driven more by this than by rational benefit | Survival |
| 3 | Social Belonging | Friendship, love, community; avoiding rejection and isolation | Social features are expected free — belonging monetizes only when exclusive | Belonging |
| 4 | Status & Recognition | Prestige, admiration, influence; modern signals: followers, likes, titles, taste | Users pay for differentiation — status is inherently scarce | Status |
| 5 | Control & Autonomy | Freedom of choice, independence, influencing one's environment | Lack of control produces stress; apps sell relief from chaos | Control |
| 6 | Competence & Progress | Skill development, mastery, measurable progress | Visible progress is the strongest retention loop | Control |
| 7 | Meaning & Purpose | Contribution, mission, values | Enables users to endure hard routines (study, training) | Curiosity |
| 8 | Identity & Self-Consistency | Self-image, alignment between actions and beliefs, personal narrative | Users defend identity — "I am a runner" retains better than "I run" | Status |
| 9 | Novelty & Curiosity | Discovery, experimentation, information seeking, variety | Users prefer safe novelty; novelty-led demand decays fastest | Curiosity |
| 10 | Fairness & Justice | Reciprocity, moral balance, sensitivity to unfair treatment | Folded into Control: fairness apps (bill splitting, price fairness, consumer rights) sell control over being wronged — note the fairness angle in the rationale when it is the dominant pull | Control |

Every desire maps to exactly one scored dimension; the aggregation table in
`demand-drivers.md` is the authoritative mapping. If a concept seems to tap
a desire outside its scored dimension, score the mapped dimension and
explain the tension in the rationale — do not invent a sixth dimension.
