## 2. FREE-TO-PAID CONVERSION

ChartMogul x ProductLed SaaS Conversion Report — 200 B2B products, Jan 2026 survey. Typical respondent: $1-10M ARR, ARPC $50-249/mo. 57% use free trial as primary entry; 14-day trial most common (62%). MEDIAN FREE-TO-PAID ACROSS ALL PRODUCTS: 8%.

| Model | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|
| Freemium (gated signup) | 3-5% | 8-12% |
| Free trial, no credit card | 4-6% | 10-15% |
| Free trial, credit card required | 25-35% | 50-60% |
| Ungated freemium | 7-9% | 8-12% |
| Reverse trial | 4-6% | 8-12% |

Per 1,000 visitors: credit-card trial 35 signups -> 10.5 paying; ungated freemium 70 -> 5.6; freemium 90 -> 5.0; no-card trial 45 -> 3.6.
Distribution is bimodal, not clustered on the median: 20% of trial products convert below 2.5%, 23% above 25%.
Sources: https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/ and https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report-2/ (2026)

First Page Sage — 86 SaaS companies (71% B2B), Q1 2022-Q3 2025, published 2025-09-05. NOTE: measures trial-STARTER -> paid, a different denominator.

| Funnel step | Organic | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in: visitor->trial | 8.5% | 7.1% |
| Opt-in: trial->paid | 18.2% | 17.4% |
| Opt-out: visitor->trial | 2.5% | 2.2% |
| Opt-out: trial->paid | 48.8% | 51% |
| Visitor->freemium | 13.3% | 15.9% |
| Freemium->paid | 2.6% | 2.8% |

Category cut from same source (trial->paid): CRM 29.0%, IoT 25.2%, Healthcare/Medtech 21.5%, Enterprise 18.6%.
Source: https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/saas-free-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks/

## 3. NET REVENUE RETENTION

| Segment | Median | Top quartile | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2B SaaS overall (ChartMogul sample) | 82% | 97% | ChartMogul AI churn wave, data through Sep 2025 |
| ARPA <$10/mo | n/a | 65.1% | ChartMogul 2023 |
| ARPA >$500/mo | n/a | 109.3% | ChartMogul 2023 |
| Private B2B, $25-50k ACV | 102% | 111% (bottom quartile 97%) | SaaS Capital, 2025-09-18 |

Only 2.7% of businesses at <$10/mo ARPA exceed 100% NRR, vs 41.1% at >$500/mo (ChartMogul 2023).
CALIBRATION POINT: ChartMogul's B2B median of 82% sits far below the 100%+ figures in VC benchmark reports because its sample skews small and self-serve. For micro-SaaS, sub-100% NRR is the norm, not a red flag.
Sources: https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/ ; https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/what-is-a-good-retention-rate-for-a-private-saas-company/

## CONFIDENCE NOTES

Well-corroborated (2+ independent sources):
- Credit-card-trial effect: ChartMogul 2026, First Page Sage 2025, Freemius 2025 all show a 3-5x uplift.
- Inverse relationship between ARPA/ACV and churn (ChartMogul + SaaS Capital).
- Freemium->paid in low single digits (ChartMogul "good" 3-5%; First Page Sage 2.6%).

Single-source:
- The ChartMogul ARPA churn table (section 1) — the only granular published cut verifiable anywhere.
- SaaS Capital ACV bands: only the $25-50k row visible outside the gated PDF. The 118%/108%/97% enterprise/mid-market/SMB split circulating online could NOT be confirmed on any SaaS Capital page — treat as unverified.
- All AI-native splits.

Major disagreement:
- Opt-in trial conversion: 18.2% (First Page Sage) vs 4-6% "good" (ChartMogul). Denominator difference (trial-starters vs all free signups) plus sample bias. For a $10-100/user/mo self-serve product, ChartMogul's numbers are the safer calibration.

Not found / deliberately excluded:
- Per-vertical churn tables exist only on SEO content farms with no stated methodology; excluded.
- MicroConf's State of Independent SaaS publishes no churn figures outside gated PDFs.
- RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps is mobile-consumer, no usable B2B slice.

Vintage: everything above is 2025-2026 EXCEPT ChartMogul ARPA-band NRR and 3-month retention (2023) and activation benchmarks (2022).
