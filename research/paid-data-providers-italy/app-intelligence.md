# App Market Intelligence Providers — Italy Coverage & Pricing (2026)

Scope: providers that can supply, via API or an affordable self-serve plan, App Store IT / Google Play IT rankings and category charts, per-app download/revenue estimates for Italy, IT-storefront keyword/ASO data, and review streams for Italian apps. Confidence tags: **high** = read directly off the provider's own current pricing/docs page; **medium** = third-party pricing-aggregator or comparison site, cross-checked against at least one other source; **low** = single third-party source, unconfirmed or possibly stale.

## Free baseline to beat

- **Apple RSS Feed Generator** (`rss.marketingtools.apple.com`) — free, no key required. Produces Top Free/Paid/Grossing charts by category, per storefront (supports `it` as a country code), JSON/XML/Atom output. No downloads, no revenue, no keyword data.
- **iTunes Search/Lookup API** (`itunes.apple.com/search`, `/lookup`) — free, no key. Metadata, pricing, ratings count, per-country availability via the `country` parameter (confirmed `IT` supported). Rate-limited to ~20 calls/min; Apple points heavy users at the paid Enterprise Partner Feed for more volume. No downloads, no revenue, no keyword volume.
- **Google Play scraping libraries** (`google-play-scraper` on PyPI, `gplay-scraper`, `RankoR/google-play-scraper`) — free, open source, no key. Scrape app metadata, reviews, search results, and top charts (`TOP_FREE`/`TOP_PAID`/`TOP_GROSSING`) per country/language, including `it`/`it`. No official install/revenue numbers (Play Console install *ranges* are visible on public listings but not exposed cleanly via these libraries), and scraping is against Google's terms of service — fine for research, risky for a production pipeline (breakage on layout changes, no SLA).
- **Apple Search Ads dashboard** — free Apple Search Ads account (no ad spend required) exposes the Search Popularity score (5–100 scale) for any keyword in any of Apple's 60+ storefronts including Italy, via the keyword planner UI or the Search Ads API (OAuth 2.0). This is the best free proxy for IT keyword-volume signal on iOS; there is no Google Play equivalent.

**What paid adds over this baseline**: (1) download and revenue *estimates* — none of the free sources provide these at all, only paid panels (Sensor Tower-class) or algorithmic estimators (Appfigures Boost+, AppTweak Market Intelligence, 42matters on quote); (2) historical keyword rank tracking over time (free sources are point-in-time only); (3) structured, rate-limit-friendly APIs instead of scraping; (4) review-stream APIs with sentiment/tagging; (5) competitor keyword-gap and ASO scoring tools.

## Per-provider notes

### Appfigures
- Self-serve tiers, all published: Starter (free) → Connect $9.99/mo → Monitor $44.99/mo → Optimize $149.99/mo → Boost $599.99/mo → Amplify $1,399.99/mo (20% off annual). Source: https://appfigures.com/platform/pricing — high confidence, fetched 2026-08-14.
- API is included starting at Connect ($9.99/mo): 1,000 requests/day free, higher tiers 2,500–50,000/day via add-on. A separate **Public Data API** (credit-metered, credits never expire) is needed for apps not linked to your own account — this is how you'd pull Italy-market data on apps you don't own. Source: https://help.appfigures.com/en/article/appfigures-api-access-limits-and-add-ons-1seiibo/ — high confidence, published 2026-07-29.
- Per-app download/revenue **estimates for apps you don't own** only unlock at Boost ($599.99/mo) and Amplify ($1,399.99/mo); below that, Appfigures reports your own connected apps' real sales data, not market-wide estimates. Confirmed on the official pricing page's feature comparison. High confidence.
- No Italy-specific pricing or coverage caveat found; storefront/country selection is a query parameter, not a plan gate.

### 42matters
- API is the core product; positioned as file-dump + API + "Explorer" UI. 14-day free trial. Source: https://42matters.com/app-market-data — high confidence (official page).
- Country coverage: 63 countries / 39 languages confirmed on the same official page, and a lookup API example response explicitly lists `"IT"` in `app_availability.available_in` — direct confirmation Italy is supported. High confidence.
- Pricing tiers (Tiny €79/mo, Small €249/mo, Medium €699/mo, Large €999/mo for API; Explorer Starter €299/mo, Professional €749/mo, Enterprise €1,149/mo; File Dumps from €998/mo) come from a third-party partner listing (swissmarte.ch, dated 2021-01-21) and are corroborated by an independent 2021 blog post citing the same figures — **medium-low confidence**, likely directionally correct but unverified against the current (2026) official pricing page, which is JavaScript-rendered and did not yield pricing text on fetch. Recommend confirming directly with 42matters before budgeting.
- 42matters was acquired by Similarweb ("42matters is now part of the Similarweb family" banner on the pricing page) — date of acquisition not established in this pass; treat current pricing as potentially revised post-acquisition. Flagged for follow-up.
- Downloads: the product APIs return metadata, rankings, and reviews; **download/install estimates** are not confirmed as a standard API product — a 42matters Italy market-statistics blog post exists (aggregated Android publisher stats) suggesting they do compute estimates, but the mechanism (API vs. custom report) wasn't confirmed. Medium-low confidence.

### AppTweak
- ASO Intelligence (keyword/rank tooling): Essential $79/mo, Grow $299/mo, Grow Plus $549–599/mo (sources disagree slightly, $549 per apptweak.com pricing snapshot vs $599 per a Sonar comparison blog — treat as ~$550–600), Enterprise custom. Source: apptweak.com/en/pricing, fetched 2026-08-14 — high confidence for Essential/Grow, medium for exact Grow Plus figure.
- Keyword database: 30M+ keywords, 100+ countries — no Italy-specific carve-out or exclusion found; standard country selector. High confidence (official page).
- **API is a separate paid product**, not bundled with ASO Intelligence: Small ~$199/mo, Medium ~$599/mo, Large ~$1,999/mo, per a vendor-pricing aggregator dated June 23, 2026, consistent with an earlier April 2024 snapshot at the same figures — medium confidence (third-party, but stable across two independent captures 2 years apart, suggesting these are genuinely static list prices). A 7-day free trial with 100,000 credits is available on the API. Source: help.apptweak.com — high confidence for the trial/credit mechanics, medium for the dollar figures.
- **Download/revenue estimates require the Market Intelligence add-on**, which is itself sales-gated (no public price) and only unlocks starting at the "Explore" plan tier of that separate product line. This is the most expensive path to Italy download/revenue numbers among the mid-market ASO tools.

### MobileAction
- ASO Intelligence: Lite $15/mo, Basic $69/mo, Pro $239/mo, Enterprise custom — all with 7-day free trials. Source: mobileaction.co/pricing — high confidence, fetched 2026-08-14.
- **API Solutions and Ad Intelligence (Apple Search Ads competitive data) are Enterprise-only** with no published price; MobileAction's own help center confirms "Only App Store Optimization offers tiered pricing plans you can review and activate directly. All other product modules are offered at the Enterprise level." High confidence (official help doc).
- Italy Apple Search Ads data: MobileAction **publishes** free annual/quarterly benchmark reports with Italy-specific figures — e.g., the 2026 benchmark report shows Italy's Search Results CPA at $1.60 (2025), the lowest of tracked regions, and a prior report shows IT CPA moving $1.38→$1.84 (2023→2024). These are free published aggregates, not raw per-app API access. High confidence for the published figures (official mobileaction.co report pages), but this is benchmark-level, not app-level, Italy data.
- MobileAction's free SearchAds.com plan (Apple Ads campaign management, not intelligence) is genuinely free up to $10,000/mo ad spend, unrelated to the app-intelligence question.

### Sensor Tower (incl. former data.ai)
- **No public price list, no self-serve signup, no free trial.** Confirmed directly on Sensor Tower's own pricing page ("We customize pricing plans for each customer — get in touch"). High confidence (official page).
- Third-party procurement-data aggregators (Vendr, SpendHound) report actual customer contract ranges: Vendr's 38 tracked transactions show a median of ~$74,415/yr, with small deployments $30K–$70K/yr, mid-market $70K–$150K/yr, enterprise $150K–$300K+/yr; SpendHound reports SMB average $79,980/yr and enterprise average $268,557/yr from 160 customer data points. Medium confidence (these are third-party transaction-data platforms, not Sensor Tower's own disclosures, but internally consistent with each other).
- data.ai (acquired by Sensor Tower, March 2024) and Apptopia (acquired by Sensor Tower, 2023) are both now folded into the same enterprise sales motion — none of the three brands offers an independent self-serve tier anymore.
- **AppMagic was acquired by Sensor Tower in May 2026** (press release dated 2026-05-12/13, corroborated by PocketGamer.biz and GamesIndustry.biz) and is being positioned explicitly as Sensor Tower's dedicated SMB product line, with Sensor Tower's CRO stating no plan to raise AppMagic's prices or sunset it as a standalone product. High confidence on the acquisition itself (multiple independent outlets, consistent dates); low-medium confidence on what AppMagic's pricing looks like post-acquisition, since no fresh public price list was found — third-party sources cite ~$150/mo for a mid tier prior to the deal, unconfirmed post-deal.
- Country-level (Italy) panel estimates almost certainly exist inside the Sensor Tower/data.ai/Apptopia combined dataset (this is their core product), but none of it is purchasable or prices without a sales conversation.

### Apptopia
- Fully absorbed into Sensor Tower's institutional-investor offering; current public-facing material (apptopia.com) markets Bloomberg Terminal integration and hedge-fund/investment-bank customers exclusively, not app publishers. No self-serve tier, no public pricing, no evidence of an API product accessible outside an institutional data contract. High confidence this is not a viable route for an indie/Italy-focused validation workflow.

### Newer/cheaper alternatives
- **AppFollow**: genuinely free tier (2 apps) that includes API access to public top-charts, category-rankings, and "what's new" endpoints — these work per-storefront including IT, at no cost, though rate/credit limits apply. Paid ASO add-on from $19/mo billed yearly (2 apps, 500 keywords). Essential $179/mo, Team $599/mo, Enterprise custom. API credit costs are published per-endpoint (e.g., top charts and category rankings cost 10 credits/request on the Free-eligible tier). Source: appfollow.io/pricing, docs.api.appfollow.io — high confidence, both official.
- **ASOdesk**: the only provider in this set with a **working free API tier** — Free plan ($0) includes 100 API credits/month, 1 app, 200 keywords. Paid tiers: Pro $59/mo (100 credits/mo — note credits don't scale linearly with price on early tiers), Guru $159/mo (1,000 credits/mo), Power $343/mo. Published per-datapoint credit-cost table (e.g., app metadata = 1 credit, keyword stats = 10 credits, estimated installs by keyword = 150 credits). 100+ countries claimed. Source: asodesk.com/api and a third-party pricing tracker (frontdeskreview.com, captured 2026-06-18) cross-checked against the official credit-cost table — medium-high confidence.
- **ASOMobile**: ASO Indie tier confirmed 500 keywords/5 apps/1,000 API credits from the official pricing page, but the dollar price for that tier did not render cleanly in this pass (page structure repeated tier names without consistently attached prices) — flagged for a follow-up direct check at asomobile.net/en/pricing/. 3-day free trial only (shorter than competitors). Medium-low confidence on exact price; the tier structure itself is high confidence (official page).

## Does not exist publicly (negative-result log)

- **No provider publishes a self-serve, itemized price for Italy-specific download/revenue *estimates*.** Every provider that offers this data point (Sensor Tower/data.ai/Apptopia/AppMagic-post-acquisition, AppTweak Market Intelligence, Appfigures Amplify's "global download estimates," 42matters' estimate capability) either gates it behind a sales call or bundles it into a top-tier subscription priced at $600–$1,400/mo minimum, with no line-item Italy surcharge or discount visible.
- **No MMP (mobile measurement partner) publishes an Italy country-cut CPI/CPA benchmark publicly** — confirmed again in this pass; the closest public substitute found was MobileAction's Apple Search Ads benchmark reports, which are Apple Ads-specific (not MMP install-attribution data) and aggregate-level, not per-app.
- **Sensor Tower's entry price is not verifiable from any authoritative source.** Sensor Tower itself publishes no number at all. Third-party procurement platforms (Vendr, SpendHound, blog aggregators) all *estimate* an entry point around $500/mo–$6,000/yr for a single module, but none of these figures come from Sensor Tower directly, and Sensor Tower's own pricing page contains zero numbers — this figure should be treated as an estimate, not a quote.
- **Apptopia has no standalone public pricing at all**, self-serve or otherwise; it is not clear it is even sold separately from a Sensor Tower/institutional-data contract any longer.
- **AppMagic's post-acquisition (May 2026) self-serve pricing is not publicly documented.** Pre-acquisition third-party estimates (~$150/mo reported mid-tier) exist but are unconfirmed as current; Sensor Tower's stated intent (no price increase, no sunset) is a public statement, not a price list.
- **42matters' current (2026) official pricing figures could not be independently confirmed** — the official pricing page is JS-rendered and returned no pricing text via fetch in this pass; only a 2021-dated third-party listing and a 2021 blog post (mutually consistent) were found. Given the confirmed Similarweb acquisition, these figures carry real risk of being stale.
- **AppTweak's exact API dollar tiers (Small/Medium/Large) could not be confirmed on AppTweak's own current pricing page** — the official page shows the credit-cost-per-datapoint mechanics and confirms three named tiers (Small/Medium/Large) plus a custom tier, but the dollar figures ($199/$599/$1,999) come only from a third-party vendor-pricing aggregator, not apptweak.com itself.
- **ASOMobile's exact dollar price for its cheapest paid ("ASO Indie") tier could not be extracted cleanly** from the official pricing page in this pass.

## Ranked recommendation

**(a) Cheapest credible route to IT chart/ranking monitoring:** the free baseline — Apple RSS Feed Generator (`rss.marketingtools.apple.com`, country=`it`) plus a Google Play scraping library (e.g. `google-play-scraper` on PyPI) for Play-side top charts. Cost: $0. If a maintained API with an SLA is wanted instead of scraping, **ASOdesk's free tier** (100 credits/mo, top charts cost 1 credit/request) is the cheapest paid-grade option, effectively also $0 for light monitoring.

**(b) Cheapest credible route to per-app IT download/revenue estimates:** none of the affordable tools (Appfigures Connect/Monitor/Optimize, AppTweak Essential/Grow, MobileAction Lite/Basic/Pro, ASOdesk, AppFollow, ASOMobile) include this at their entry price — it is uniformly gated to the upper tiers. The cheapest *confirmed, published* price for this specific capability is **Appfigures Boost at $599.99/mo** ($449.99/mo annual), which unlocks unlimited download/revenue estimates for apps you don't own, across any storefront including Italy. AppTweak's Market Intelligence add-on may be cheaper but has no public price to compare. Below $600/mo, expect only "estimates for your own connected apps," not market-wide competitor numbers — this confirms the prior research finding that Italy-level estimates live only in paid panels.

**(c) Cheapest credible route to IT ASO keyword data:** **ASOdesk Free** ($0, 200 keywords, 100 API credits/mo) or **AppFollow's free tier + $19/mo ASO add-on** (billed yearly, 500 keywords, all-ranked-keywords, keyword-difficulty history) are the two cheapest working options with API access. For volume beyond ~500 keywords, **MobileAction Basic at $69/mo** (500 keywords, 8 apps) or **AppTweak Essential at $79/mo** (500 keywords, no API) are the next step, with AppTweak's much larger 30M-keyword/100+-country database as the differentiator if keyword coverage breadth matters more than price.

**Negative-result highlights for the pack:** Sensor Tower/data.ai/Apptopia are enterprise-only with no verifiable entry price (third-party estimates only, ~$30K–$300K+/yr); AppMagic was acquired by Sensor Tower in May 2026, with intent to preserve it as an affordable SMB line but no confirmed current public pricing; 42matters' 2026 pricing could not be independently verified (only 2021-dated figures found) despite being the strongest fit on paper for Italy-cut metadata/rankings/reviews via API.
